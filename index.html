Card Reaper Custom Art Studio

Instructions

Click and drag to draw, right-click and drag to erase. Change width and height to desired. Once you're happy with your picture, click "Image->Json" and copy the json string for your card.

If someone sends you a card in json form, you can paste it in the input/output field, then click "Json->Image" to display how the card will look in-game.
